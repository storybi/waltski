Snow-level headaches: a real storm, but only the peaks turn white

OpenSnow forecaster Evan Thayer expects another storm this weekend into early next week, but the warm air ahead of it will be slow to clear out, so snow levels stay high and drop only a little. HBR · Risk
Mixed

AI-Powered Fraud Slips Through the Gaps Between Departments

An FBI agent, writing in a personal capacity, argues that cheap AI fakes of trusted voices, faces and documents let fraudsters exploit the handoffs between functions even when each system is secure on its own. He compares it to the 1963 salad-oil scandal, where lenders trusted warehouse receipts until about $175 million of collateral turned out not to exist. His advice: trace high-risk payments from start to finish, give each handoff a clear owner, confirm requests through channels the requester can't control, and let designated staff stop suspicious payments.

Phase 3 · Lung cancer

Adding Imdelltra to Imfinzi extends survival by eight months in small cell lung cancer

In the 563-patient DeLLphi-305 trial presented at ESMO in Madrid, people with extensive-stage small cell lung cancer who got Amgen's Imdelltra (tarlatamab) plus Imfinzi lived a median 21.1 months, vs. 12.7 months on Imfinzi alone, a 58% lower risk of death. About 80% were alive at one year vs. 53%. Imdelltra is already approved for other uses, but not yet for this one.

ESMO · Melanoma vaccine

Moderna and Merck's personalized cancer vaccine cuts melanoma recurrence by 42%

Full data from a large trial show that intismeran autogene, an mRNA vaccine built from each patient's own tumor mutations and given with Keytruda, lowered the risk of melanoma returning by 42% compared with standard treatment, and patients stayed cancer-free significantly longer. Experts are still debating how widely the custom-made approach will carry over to other cancers.

Phase 3 · Multiple sclerosis

Novartis pill beats an approved MS drug on relapses in two pivotal trials

In the REMODEL-1 and REMODEL-2 studies of about 2,000 people with relapsing MS, oral remibrutinib cut the yearly relapse rate by 35.2% and 31.2% compared with Sanofi's Aubagio, with what was described as a cleaner safety profile. Its edge is smaller than the more-than-50% cut reported for Roche's rival fenebrutinib.

Brain-guided magnetic stimulation shows early promise for PTSD

A Texas A&M study of Magnetic e-Resonance Therapy, which uses EEG readings and an AI-assisted algorithm to tailor magnetic pulses to each patient, reported a 52% drop in PTSD symptoms and 68% remission after five to six weeks of daily sessions. UK regulators find AI agents everywhere in finance, but on a short leash

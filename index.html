Better clarity on a more active pattern: first storm Saturday, more next week

Two more sunny days, then a stormy Saturday and a snowy turn by Tuesday

The NWS forecast for Morgan stays sunny and warm through Thursday (highs 63–64°), turns partly cloudy Friday, then brings a stormy Saturday with a 94% chance of precipitation and highs falling to about 50°, with snow back in the valley forecast by Tuesday. Consumer prices rose 0.4% in October and core prices 0.3%, both a tenth above forecasts, as services, food and gasoline all pushed higher. AI Is Quietly Turning America Back Toward Free Trade

The Economist finds that the AI build-out has kept global trade booming despite tariffs: world trade is up more than 27% in dollar terms since late 2024, and US imports of AI hardware jumped 69% in the first eight months of 2026 while all other imports fell 10%. Most servers and chips slipped through tariff exemptions, and with roughly two-thirds of midterm voters blaming tariffs for hurting the economy, the paper argues Washington may lean further toward open trade.

How CEOs Set the Tempo

Harvard Business School’s Nitin Nohria argues that the pace at which a chief executive drives change is a strategic choice, not a personality trait. Favorable
FDA approval · Joint tumors

FDA approves pimicotinib (Pimjetmi) for tenosynovial giant cell tumor

The once-daily CSF-1R blocker is now cleared for people with symptomatic tenosynovial giant cell tumor, a rare growth in the joints, when surgery could cause serious harm or loss of function. In the placebo-controlled phase 3 MANEUVER trial, 76.2% of 63 treated patients saw their tumors shrink on independent review, and the median length of response had not yet been reached.

OncLive · Nov 10, 2026

FDA approval · Stroke prevention

FDA approves Bayer’s Sunharbo, a new type of blood thinner, to prevent repeat strokes

Sunharbo (asundexian) blocks clotting factor XIa and is cleared for adults after a recent mild-to-moderate non-cardioembolic ischemic stroke or a high-risk TIA. In the phase 3 OCEANIC-STROKE trial of 12,327 patients, adding it to standard antiplatelet therapy cut ischemic strokes from 8.4% to 6.2%, a 26% reduction, without raising bleeding risk.

FDA · Nov 9, 2026

Phase 3 safety trial · Obesity

Boehringer’s obesity drug survodutide clears its heart-safety test at AHA 2026

SYNCHRONIZE-CVOT enrolled 5,508 higher-risk adults with obesity or overweight and found no rise in major cardiac events versus placebo (hazard ratio 0.84), meeting its safety goal but not proving heart protection. At 72 weeks, weight fell 13.7% on the 6 mg dose and 12.2% on 3.6 mg versus 2% on placebo; Boehringer is targeting a 2027 filing.

Phase 3 trial · Hepatitis delta

Vir’s monthly two-drug shot clears hepatitis delta virus in most patients in phase 3

In the open-label ECLIPSE 1 trial of 124 adults, presented at The Liver Meeting, 79% of those on elebsiran plus tobevibart had undetectable virus at 48 weeks versus none in the delayed-treatment group. The combined goal of no detectable virus plus normal liver enzymes was reached by 44% versus 0%, with side effects mostly mild; results are sponsor-reported and there was no active comparator.

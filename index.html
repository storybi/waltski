OpenSnow · Utah Daily Snow · Nov 12
Favorable

Three storms, three chances: an active week-plus pattern starts tonight

In Thursday’s Utah Daily Snow, “3 Storms = 3 Opportunities for Snow,” Evan Thayer says the active pattern arrives today and keeps going for at least a week, with three separate systems lined up. Early snow levels are high, so the upper mountains benefit first, but he says significant accumulations are possible at high elevations over the next 10 days, the best base-building setup yet ahead of Snowbasin’s Nov 27 opening.

Source: OpenSnow Utah Daily Snow (Nov 12)
Northern Wasatch · Snowbasin point forecast · Nov 13
Mixed

Rain at the base tonight and Saturday, a dry Sunday and Monday, then all snow by Tuesday night

The NWS forecast for Snowbasin’s base area (6,350 ft) is mostly cloudy today with rain after 5 p.m., turning to rain and snow overnight and through Saturday with little or no accumulation at that elevation. Sunday and Monday are mostly sunny; rain and snow return Tuesday and change to all snow Tuesday night, with snow likely again Wednesday night. SnowBrains expects the weekend storm to drop 6–9 inches on the upper Cottonwoods, so the higher terrain gains while the Snowbasin base stays bare for now.

Sources: NWS Salt Lake City point forecast, Snowbasin · SnowBrains (Nov 12)

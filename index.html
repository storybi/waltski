Northern Wasatch · NWS Salt Lake City · Nov 14
Favorable

Valley rain, mountain snow today; a colder storm Tuesday could bring snow to the benches

Today’s fast-moving system brings rain in the valleys (Morgan near 47°) and snow up high, with snow levels falling to about 6,000–7,000 feet behind the front; NWS guidance gives the mountains about 4–8 inches, more in the upper Cottonwoods. After a dry Sunday and Monday, the next storm around Nov 17–18 is colder, with snow levels possibly down to the higher valleys, and Morgan’s forecast shows snow Tuesday through Friday, all ahead of Snowbasin’s tentative Nov 27 opening.

FDA approves Imfinzi plus enfortumab vedotin around surgery for cisplatin-ineligible bladder cancer

Kindness Instead

“Not rendering evil for evil.” (1 Peter 3:9, KJV)

Patricia Raybon’s devotional for today centers on Peter’s call to answer insults and hurts with blessing rather than payback. Choosing kindness when we’re wronged isn’t weakness; it reflects the grace we’ve received, and Scripture promises that those who bless will inherit a blessing.

Not rendering evil for evil, or railing for railing: but contrariwise blessing; knowing that ye are thereunto called, that ye should inherit a blessing.1 Peter 3:9 (KJV)

No Divided Heart

“Swear by the Lord, and that swear by Malcham.”

Spurgeon warns against trying to serve the Lord and an idol at once, as the people in Zephaniah’s day did; God refuses a divided loyalty, and those who keep a foot in both camps only deceive themselves. He presses each reader to ask honestly whether Christ has the whole heart.

And them that worship the host of heaven upon the housetops; and them that worship and that swear by the LORD, and that swear by Malcham.Zephaniah 1:5 (KJV)

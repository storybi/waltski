Our Daily Bread · Oct 27

A Rich Life of Gratitude

“It is only with gratitude that life becomes rich.”

Writing from prison in 1943, Dietrich Bonhoeffer observed that gratitude is what makes a life rich, and Paul, chained for the gospel, opens his letter to Timothy with thanks (2 Timothy 1:1–3, 6–10). Neither man was spared hardship, yet both leaned on a God whose grace gives far more than we could ever repay, which invites us to notice and thank those whose care has shaped us.

Who hath saved us, and called us with an holy calling, not according to our works, but according to his own purpose and grace, which was given us in Christ Jesus before the world began.2 Timothy 1:9 (KJV)

Spurgeon · Morning and Evening · Oct 27

Treasure Up the Faithful Sayings

“Treasure up these faithful sayings.”

Spurgeon notes that Paul marks four statements in his letters as “faithful sayings” and reads them as a progression: salvation rests on God’s free grace, godliness carries promise for this life and the next, those who suffer with Christ will reign with Him, and believers are to show their faith in steady good works. He urges us to keep them close as a guide, a comfort and a daily instruction.

It is a faithful saying: For if we be dead with him, we shall also live with him.2 Timothy 2:11 (KJV)

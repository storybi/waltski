Watch & wait: high pressure parks over Utah

Evan Thayer says high pressure takes over for at least the next week, with little to no snow for northern Utah and no accumulations in the forecast. He is watching for a possible pattern change around mid-month that could bring snow chances back, though confidence in the timing is still low.

Source: OpenSnow Utah Daily Snow
Northern Wasatch · NWS Salt Lake City · Nov 6 morning
Unfavorable

Dry and warming into next week; snowmaking window closing

A weak system may graze northern Utah today with little or no accumulation, and the Snowbasin stake shows no new snow. Then high pressure warms things from about 5° below normal to 5–10° above normal early next week: Morgan sunny near 54° today and 57–64° Saturday through Monday, with lows near 30°. The NWS sees the next low-confidence chance of precipitation around Nov 12–13; Snowbasin still targets a Nov 27 opening, so tonight's and Saturday's cold hours are the best snowmaking time.

Sources: NWS Salt Lake City forecast discussion · OnTheSnow projected openings

FDA approval · Prostate cancer

FDA approves Pfizer's Talzenna plus Xtandi for earlier-stage metastatic prostate cancer

The approval covers hormone-sensitive metastatic prostate cancer with HRR gene mutations and rests on the phase 3 TALAPRO-3 trial of 599 men. Adding Talzenna cut the risk of progression or death by 52% versus Xtandi alone; median progression-free survival has not yet been reached on the combination, versus 45.8 months with Xtandi.

CURE · Oct 26, 2026

FDA approval · Respiratory papillomatosis

FDA grants accelerated approval to Inovio's Voceryq, the first plasmid DNA immunotherapy

Voceryq is approved for adults with recurrent respiratory papillomatosis, an HPV-driven disease that grows benign tumors in the airway and often needs repeated surgeries. Given as an intramuscular injection with Inovio's CELLECTRA device, it trains the immune system against HPV types 6 and 11; orders open in about 60 days, with a full launch planned for early 2027.

FDA · Inovio · Oct 30, 2026

FDA approval · Psoriatic arthritis

FDA approves Sun Pharma's Ilumya for adults with active psoriatic arthritis

The IL-23 blocker, approved for plaque psoriasis since 2018, now also covers psoriatic arthritis after the phase 3 INSPIRE-1 and INSPIRE-2 trials beat placebo on 24-week ACR20 response. Dosing is 100 mg at weeks 0 and 4, then every 12 weeks, which makes it one of the least frequent injections in the class.

Healio · Oct 30, 2026

Small trial · Type 2 diabetes

Low-calorie diet plus exercise puts 54% of young adults' type 2 diabetes into remission

The RESET for Remission trial randomized 96 adults aged 18–45 with early-onset type 2 diabetes in the UK and Canada. After 24 weeks of an 800–900 calorie-a-day diet with supervised exercise, 54% were in remission versus about 4% on usual care, with 8.4 kg of weight loss versus 1.2 kg; the study was small and short, so lasting benefit is unproven.

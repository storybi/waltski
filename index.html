FDA approval · Vitiligo

Upadacitinib becomes the first systemic pill approved for vitiligo

The FDA approved AbbVie’s Rinvoq 15 mg daily for nonsegmental vitiligo in patients 12 and older, based on two phase 3 Viti-Up trials in 614 patients. At 48 weeks, 23% to 25% of treated patients regained at least 75% of facial pigment versus 6% to 7% on placebo.

FDA approval · Early breast cancer

Giredestrant cleared as adjuvant therapy for early HR-positive breast cancer

The FDA approved Roche’s oral SERD giredestrant (Wilserdly) for stage 1–3 HR-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer after surgery. In the 4,170-patient lidERA trial it cut the risk of invasive recurrence by 30% versus standard endocrine therapy at about 32 months.

FDA approval · Turner syndrome

Weekly somapacitan approved for growth failure in Turner syndrome

The FDA approved Novo Nordisk’s once-weekly Sogroya for children 2.5 and older, sparing families daily growth-hormone shots. In the phase 3 REAL8 trial, weekly dosing matched daily somatropin on height velocity at 52 weeks (about 9.0 vs. 9.5 cm a year) with similar safety.

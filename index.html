Favorable

Friday–Saturday storm opens a three-storm stretch, with snow levels falling as it goes

In Wednesday’s Utah Daily Snow, Evan Thayer says the first storm reaches Utah late Friday and lasts through Saturday, starting with high snow levels so the snow sticks mainly to the upper mountains. A second storm looks possible around midweek and a third later next week into next weekend; as colder air works in and snow levels drop, he says the series could add up to significant accumulation, the best setup yet for Snowbasin’s Nov 27 opening.

Mixed

Two more dry days, a rainy Saturday at the base, then rain and snow by Tuesday night

The NWS forecast for Snowbasin’s base area (6,350 ft) is sunny and 54° today and Friday, then rain arrives after 11 p.m. Friday (80%) and Saturday is rain possibly mixed with snow, a 100% chance with a high near 41° but little or no snow accumulation at that elevation. The base is still bare, so the real gains this weekend will be up high.

The Economist finds the rich world has passed “peak boomer”: in 26 of 32 wealthy countries, the incomes of the elderly are now falling relative to those of working-age people. Favorable
FDA approval · Multiple myeloma

FDA grants accelerated approval to iberdomide (Zenbexus), a first-in-class myeloma drug

Iberdomide, taken with daratumumab and dexamethasone, is now cleared for adults whose multiple myeloma returned after at least one prior line including a proteasome inhibitor and an immunomodulatory drug. It is the first approved cereblon E3 ligase modulator and the first myeloma approval based on measurable residual disease: 41% reached MRD-negative complete response versus 21% on the standard DVd combination.

Phase 3 trial · Psoriatic arthritis

A daily IL-23 pill, icotrokinra, eases psoriatic arthritis in phase 3

In the ICONIC-PsA 1 trial, 58% of biologic-naive patients on 200 mg once daily reached an ACR20 response at 16 weeks versus 41% on placebo; a 400 mg dose added no extra benefit, and no new safety signals appeared. It could give patients an oral option where IL-23 blockers today are injections.

Phase 3 trial · Sjögren’s disease

Amgen’s dazodalibep becomes a likely first therapy aimed at Sjögren’s disease activity

In the phase 3 OASIZ-301 study, both monthly and every-12-week doses cut systemic disease activity at week 48 (ESSDAI down 5.6 and 5.2 points vs. 3.8 on placebo), and the monthly dose also eased patient-reported dryness. Side effects were mostly mild to moderate; results are company-reported ahead of full publication.

The Trial of Your Faith

“Tempests are her trainers, and lightnings are her illuminators.”

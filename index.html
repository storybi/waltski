OpenSnow · Utah Daily Snow · Nov 16
Favorable

Two storms in five days could add up to feet of snow before Thanksgiving

The first storm moves in late Monday and lasts into Wednesday, with the steadiest snow Tuesday afternoon into Wednesday morning; a colder second storm follows Thursday and Friday. Across both systems the Utah mountains could pick up roughly 2 to 5 feet, the kind of base-building run that sets up Thanksgiving openings.

Northern Wasatch · NWS winter weather advisory · Nov 17
Favorable

Advisory from 11 a.m. today: 4–8 inches up high, 1–4 inches in Ogden Valley

The NWS Salt Lake City office has a Winter Weather Advisory for the Wasatch Mountains and Wasatch Back from 11 a.m. Tuesday to 11 a.m. Wednesday: 4–8 inches above 8,000 feet (a foot or more in the upper Cottonwoods) and 1–4 inches for the Wasatch Back, with Ogden Valley and Park City on the high end. Snow levels drop to the valley benches Tuesday evening, and the colder Thursday–Friday storm should stack more on top ahead of Snowbasin’s planned Nov 27 opening.

Sources: NWS Salt Lake City, Snowbasin forecast & advisory (Nov 17) · OpenSnow Utah

NEJM trial · Heart block

Left bundle branch pacing beats standard pacing for heart block

In the international OptimPacing trial, pacing the heart’s left bundle branch cut the combined risk of death, heart-failure hospitalization or pacing-induced cardiomyopathy by 59% versus right-ventricular pacing: 8.5% versus 19.2% at four years. No left-bundle patients developed pacing-induced cardiomyopathy, compared with 11.7% of the others.

The dissolving film (dexmedetomidine) can now be taken at home by adults with agitation from schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder or bipolar I or II, instead of only under medical supervision, making it the first such treatment patients or caregivers can give themselves. Early trial · Atrial fibrillation

Sacubitril/valsartan may cut AF recurrence after ablation

In the open-label SUPERIOR study of 320 patients in China with atrial fibrillation and high blood pressure, starting sacubitril/valsartan soon after ablation lowered 12-month recurrence to 18.0% versus 28.3% on valsartan alone (HR 0.58). Blood pressure fell equally in both arms; investigators call the result preliminary pending larger blinded trials.

PayPal runs $53 billion in treasury cash through a conversational AI agent

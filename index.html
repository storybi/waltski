Snowbasin opened its 86th season to a Black Friday crowd on a 21-inch base; its stake read 0" at 4 a.m. ahead of today’s cold front. FDA approval · Flu vaccine

FDA clears Moderna’s mRNA flu shot for adults 50 and older

Moderna’s mFLUSIVA is the first mRNA seasonal flu vaccine approved in the US, cleared for adults 50 and older. In a trial of 50- to 64-year-olds it led to 27% fewer confirmed flu cases than a standard shot, with more (mostly mild, short-lived) side effects; Moderna will submit data comparing it with high-dose vaccines for people 65 and up.

ABC News · Nov 27, 2026
Favorable
FDA approval · Breast cancer

Giredestrant becomes a new hormone pill after early breast cancer surgery

The FDA approved Genentech’s oral estrogen-receptor blocker giredestrant as adjuvant therapy for ER-positive, HER2-negative stage I–III breast cancer at intermediate to high risk of recurrence. In the 4,170-patient lidERA trial, 92.4% were free of invasive disease at three years versus 89.6% on standard endocrine therapy (a 30% risk reduction); survival data are still immature.

Oncology Central · Nov 27, 2026

FDA approval · GIST

First new second-line option for GIST in 20 years: bezuclastinib plus sunitinib

Cogent’s bezuclastinib (Ekitna) won approval with sunitinib for advanced gastrointestinal stromal tumors after imatinib, six days ahead of its PDUFA date. In the 413-patient phase 3 PEAK trial, median progression-free survival was 16.5 months versus 9.2 on sunitinib alone, though the label carries a boxed liver-toxicity warning.

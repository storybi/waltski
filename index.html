This week · Oct 26–Nov 1
Mixed

Dry, mild days now; a wetter, colder turn for the weekend

NWS Salt Lake City has Morgan mostly sunny through Thursday with highs near 60, broken only by chances of valley rain Monday night (40%) and Tuesday night (50%). Rain becomes likely Friday through Sunday (60–70%) with valley lows in the mid-30s, so snow is possible on the upper mountain. OpenSnow expects a weak system to brush Utah midweek and a stronger storm possible next weekend.

Sources: NWS Salt Lake City · OpenSnow Snowbasin
Recap · Oct 22–25
Mixed

Second storm of the season drops a few more inches up high

A soaking round of rain and mountain snow moved through Thursday night into Friday — on top of the season's first real snow from Tropical Storm Rachel's remnants Oct 12–15, which buried Little Cottonwood Canyon (18 inches at Snowbird, 16 at Alta) while leaving northern Utah resorts comparatively bare. This week's round added 2–6 inches above 9,000 feet in the Wasatch and Uintas before clearing. October has been wet, but most of the water soaked into the ground: no Wasatch SNOTEL site holds more than an inch of snow-water equivalent.

Sources: NWS Salt Lake City · KPCW · Wasatch Weather Weenies
8–14 days · Nov 1–7
Mixed

No clear storm signal yet for the Rockies

NOAA's Climate Prediction Center's newest 8–14 day guidance (valid Nov 6–12) leans above-normal temperatures and below-normal precipitation across most of the West, with no specific heavy-snow call for Utah. That's consistent with — not a contradiction of — the pattern that brought a Halloween-week storm mainly to the southern Rockies and Sierra; the picture should sharpen as the window gets closer.

Source: NOAA CPC 8–14 Day Outlook
Season · Winter 2026–27
Favorable

Super El Niño favors a big Wasatch winter — mostly Jan–Mar

With a potentially record El Niño building and the ECMWF seasonal model locked onto it, OpenSnow favors above-normal snow for the Wasatch, including Snowbasin and Park City, with the best odds January–March and no clear early-season signal. Northern Utah may run warm with occasional high snow levels. Colorado's south and east are favored in Nov–Dec and spring.

Sources: OpenSnow winter preview · Severe Weather Europe

God Knows Your Name

“Someone much more important already knows your name and already sees you.”

Writing to exiles in Babylon, Isaiah carried God's promise that He had called His people by name and would bring them home (Isaiah 43:1–7). The reading, which opens with a charity auction where bidders paid to have their names in a novel, reminds us that being known by God matters far more, so we need not fear an uncertain future.

Spurgeon · Morning and Evening · Oct 26

Giving by Bushels, Receiving by Cartloads

“He gives by cartloads to those who give by bushels.”

Through Haggai, God told a people who had let His house lie in ruins while they tended their own that this was why their harvests kept shrinking. Spurgeon calls holding back from God’s work a false economy: stinginess tends to bring the very want it fears, while an open hand, offered in faith, is met by a God who can multiply what we do or quietly blow it away.

Ye looked for much, and, lo, it came to little; and when ye brought it home, I did blow upon it. Why? saith the LORD of hosts. Because of mine house that is waste, and ye run every man unto his own house.Haggai 1:9 (KJV)

Immunotherapy around surgery extends survival for patients who can't take cisplatin

FDA approves Imfinzi plus enfortumab vedotin around surgery for cisplatin-ineligible bladder cancer

The approval covers muscle-invasive bladder cancer in patients who can’t take or decline cisplatin. In the 695-patient phase 3 VOLGA trial, the combination cut the risk of recurrence or progression by 49% and the risk of death by 42%; two-year survival was 78% versus 69%.

Tumor Treating Fields delay pain and quality-of-life decline in pancreatic cancer

A PANOVA-3 analysis in JAMA Network Open found that adding Novocure’s TTFields device to chemotherapy for locally advanced pancreatic cancer delayed worsening of pain (10.1 vs 7.4 months) and of overall health status (7.1 vs 5.7 months).

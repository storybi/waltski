FDA approvals · Myasthenia gravis

FDA approves two new drugs for generalized myasthenia gravis

The FDA cleared gefurulimab (Klygefa) and cemdisiran (Livsayis) for adults with AChR antibody-positive generalized myasthenia gravis. In their phase 3 trials, gefurulimab improved daily-living (MG-ADL) scores by 4.2 points at 26 weeks versus 2.6 on placebo, and cemdisiran by 4.5 points at 24 weeks versus 2.2.

Neladalkib wins accelerated approval for previously treated ALK-positive lung cancer

The FDA granted accelerated approval to neladalkib (Nelkarmi), a once-daily 150 mg pill, for ALK-positive non-small cell lung cancer after earlier ALK inhibitors. In the ALKOVE-1 trial, 49% of patients treated only with alectinib before responded, and 32% of heavily pretreated patients did.

Phase 3 · Pancreatic cancer

Daraxonrasib nearly doubles survival in previously treated pancreatic cancer

In Revolution Medicines’ phase 3 RASolute 302 trial, the once-daily RAS-inhibitor pill extended median overall survival to 13.2 months versus 6.7 months on chemotherapy (a 60% lower risk of death) and doubled progression-free survival to 7.2 months. About 30% of patients responded versus 10% on chemo, with mostly mild side effects; FDA and EMA reviews are reportedly under way.

Oral Alzheimer’s drug AR1001 slows decline 31% in a subgroup, misses overall

AriBio’s 78-week POLARIS-AD data showed AR1001 slowed decline on CDR-SB by 31% versus placebo in the 604-patient monotherapy group, but only 7% across all 1,535 patients, which was not statistically significant.

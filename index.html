OpenSnow · Utah Daily Snow · Nov 17
Favorable

Heavy mountain snow today, snow levels crash tonight, then a second storm Thursday–Friday

Evan Thayer had heavy mountain snow building by midday Tuesday, with snow levels starting above 7,000 feet and dropping to 5,000 feet or lower by evening as a second wave moved through. The next storm arrives Thursday and runs into Friday with more significant accumulation; storm-cycle forecasts through Saturday run about 21–30 inches for Powder Mountain and Park City, 17–24 inches for Deer Valley and up to 3 feet or more in the Cottonwoods.

Source: OpenSnow Utah Daily Snow (Nov 17) · OpenSnow US & Canada (Nov 18)
Northern Wasatch · Storm totals & openings · Nov 18
Favorable

About 7 inches on the Snowbasin stake, with feet more due before Thanksgiving

Snowbasin’s snow stake read about 7 inches of new snow early Wednesday after rain turned to snow down to the benches overnight, and the hourly outlook brings another round from Thursday into Friday. Resorts are still pacing their openers to build base: Alta pushed to Nov 25 and Park City to Saturday, citing thin early cover and warm snowmaking nights, while Snowbasin is still aiming for Nov 27.

Wegovy cuts BMI in children ages 6 to 11 in the STEP Young trial

Ridge holds Utah dry and mild this week; snow chances return around Nov 16–18

In the newest Utah Daily Snow post (Sunday evening), Evan Thayer keeps high pressure in charge for the week ahead, meaning quiet, warmer-than-normal days and no meaningful mountain snow for northern Utah. Northern Wasatch · Snowbasin forecast & report · Nov 10
Favorable

First real storm in weeks penciled in for Saturday at Snowbasin

The NOAA forecast on Snowbasin’s mountain report now shows sunny, mild days in the mid-40s through Thursday, clouds Friday, then moderate to heavy snow on Saturday, Nov 14, with highs dropping near 39°; another wet system follows around Monday, Nov 16, though it may fall as rain at lower elevations. That would be welcome: Snowbasin still has a bare base, just 2 inches for the season and a snowpack near 18% of average, with its Nov 27 opener (weather permitting) 17 days away; Powder Mountain is targeting Dec 11.

Sources: Snowbasin Mountain Report (NOAA forecast) · OpenSnow Snowbasin snow report · TownLift, Nov 6

AI Is Quietly Turning America Back Toward Free Trade

The Economist finds that the AI build-out has kept global trade booming despite tariffs: world trade is up more than 27% in dollar terms since late 2024, and US imports of AI hardware jumped 69% in the first eight months of 2026 while all other imports fell 10%. Most servers and chips slipped through tariff exemptions, and with roughly two-thirds of midterm voters blaming tariffs for hurting the economy, the paper argues Washington may lean further toward open trade.

Mixed

When Rising Interest Rates Signal Economic Strength—and When They Signal Risk

BCG economists Philipp Carlsson-Szlezak and Paul Swartz note the 10-year Treasury yield hit 5.30% in early October, more than 400 basis points above early 2020, and warn executives schooled in the low-rate 2010s not to assume high rates spell doom. Their advice: work out whether rates are rising for good, bad or ugly reasons, check customers’ debt and floating-rate exposure, watch financing strain at smaller firms, and weigh borrowing costs against capacity needs instead of reflexively delaying investment.

FDA approval · Stroke prevention

FDA approves Bayer’s Sunharbo, a new type of blood thinner, to prevent repeat strokes

Sunharbo (asundexian) blocks clotting factor XIa and is cleared for adults after a recent mild-to-moderate non-cardioembolic ischemic stroke or a high-risk TIA. In the phase 3 OCEANIC-STROKE trial of 12,327 patients, adding it to standard antiplatelet therapy cut ischemic strokes from 8.4% to 6.2%, a 26% reduction, without raising bleeding risk.

The FDA gave tentative approval to Dr. Reddy’s generic version of Gilead’s Yeztugo, which lists for about $28,218 a year in the US. Novartis’ pelacarsen slashes Lp(a) by 78% but fails to prevent heart attacks or strokes

Lp(a)HORIZON, presented at AHA 2026 and published in NEJM, followed 8,350 heart patients with high lipoprotein(a) for a median 4.7 years. J&J’s nipocalimab shows lupus benefit by one year in a mid-stage trial

In the 228-patient JASMINE-SLE study presented at ACR Convergence, the 15 mg/kg dose was not clearly better than placebo at the 24-week primary check (53.5% vs. 46.7% response).

Storm winds down; weak systems this week, a better shot at month’s end

Snowbasin’s base reaches 21 inches ahead of its Black Friday opener

OnTheSnow now lists 21 inches at Snowbasin (Powder Mountain 16), and Ski Utah expects Snowbasin to open Friday, Nov. 27, after Brighton (Tuesday) and Alta and Snowbird (Wednesday); Alta reports a 60-inch base after storms of 10, 19 and 5 inches. Phase 3 · Pancreatic cancer

Daraxonrasib nearly doubles survival in previously treated pancreatic cancer

In Revolution Medicines’ phase 3 RASolute 302 trial, the once-daily RAS-inhibitor pill extended median overall survival to 13.2 months versus 6.7 months on chemotherapy (a 60% lower risk of death) and doubled progression-free survival to 7.2 months. About 30% of patients responded versus 10% on chemo, with mostly mild side effects; FDA and EMA reviews are reportedly under way.

Phase 3 · Hepatitis delta

Two-drug shot regimen clears hepatitis delta virus in most patients

In the 124-patient ECLIPSE 1 trial, monthly elebsiran plus tobevibart made the virus undetectable in 79% of treated patients by week 48 (85% of those with cirrhosis), and 44% also normalized liver enzymes, versus none in the delayed-treatment group. Hepatitis delta is the most severe form of viral hepatitis and has no approved U.S. treatment; results came from The Liver Meeting.
